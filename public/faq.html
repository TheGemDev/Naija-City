Naija City FAQ

Questions players ask about the free Nigerian life sim

What is Naija City?

Naija City is a free 3D Nigerian life simulation game. You live in Kaduna, Abuja, Lagos or Kano with other real players: work, hustle, buy land, build houses, start businesses, raise a family and run for office.

Is Naija City free?

Yes. Naija City is free to play in your browser. The game is also open source.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Naija City runs in the browser on your phone or computer. You can add it to your home screen to open it like an app and get notifications.

Is there an Android or iPhone app?

Not in the app stores. Open naija.city in your phone’s browser and add it to your home screen to get the app on your phone.

How do I sign in?

Tap Play to start as a guest straight away, or sign in with Google or email. If you start as a guest, link Google or email in Settings to keep your player safe.

Which cities are in the game?

Four Nigerian cities: Kaduna, Abuja, Lagos and Kano. Each has its own real landmarks, markets, estates and jobs. You travel between them by plane or train.

How do I make money in Naija City?

Get a job and work shifts, claim your daily check-in, do phone and street gigs, farm, open a business, rent out houses, invest, and invite friends for a referral bonus.

Can I withdraw the money I make in the game?

No. Naira and everything else you earn in Naija City is virtual. It has no real-world cash value and can’t be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.

How do I build a house?

Buy a plot in one of the estates, pick a house design and a contractor, then pay for each stage: foundation, blockwork, roofing and finishing. When it is finished you can furnish it, rent it out or sell it.

Can I become governor or president?

Yes. Elections hold every 3 days in the INEC app. File for President, a governorship, FCT Minister, IGP or EFCC Chair, campaign, and win the vote to set real rules for every player.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Everyone plays in the same world. Send your friends your invite link, then chat, hang out, throw owambes, play Ludo or Whot together, or even get married.

Who can play Naija City?

Naija City is for adults 18 and over.

Is Naija City the same as Lagos Life?

No. Naija City is a separate game with its own cities, characters and features, including Kaduna, Abuja and Kano as well as Lagos.
